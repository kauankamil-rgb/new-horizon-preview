Bathroom Remodel in Aliso Viejo

Aliso Viejo, Orange CountyBathroom Remodeling

A jetted tub and a corner shower came out. A walk-in shower with a frameless sliding door and a black double vanity with brushed gold fixtures went in. One before photo, five after.

Get a Free Estimate (949) 939‑6445

What changed

One wall became a walk-in shower

Before, the back wall of this Aliso Viejo bathroom held a jetted tub on a beige tile deck under three small high windows, and a corner shower behind a framed metal door. Both came out.

The right part of that wall is now a walk-in shower. A full-height wall separates it from an open alcove under the first window. The walls are now a light gray-blue and the floor is wood-look plank.

Across from the shower sits the new vanity: black, double, with a white countertop with gray veining and brushed gold fittings. The list on the right covers the rest of what the after photos show.

In the after photos

What went in

  • Shower. Large-format tile walls, a pebble tile floor with a linear drain and a full-height partition wall.
  • Fittings. A rain shower head on a wall arm, a handheld sprayer on a slide bar and a two-tier recessed niche with a brushed metal bar below it.
  • Door. Frameless sliding glass with brushed metal hardware.
  • Floor and walls. Light wood-look plank flooring and light gray-blue walls.
  • Vanity. A black double vanity with two undermount sinks and a white countertop with gray veining.
  • Finish. Brushed gold widespread faucets and pulls, gold-framed mirrors and matching gold three-light fixtures.
